# Privacy Policy — StringWise (Learn Violin)

**Last updated:** [add date when you publish]

This policy explains what StringWise does — and does not do — with your data.

## Microphone access

StringWise uses your device's microphone for two features: the Tuner and the
Live Finger Placement Check. Both work by analyzing the pitch of sound in
real time, directly on your device.

- Audio is processed **locally, in memory, on your device only.**
- Audio is **never recorded, stored, saved to a file, or transmitted** to us
  or to any third party.
- We do not have servers that receive microphone data. There is nothing to
  send it to.
- Microphone access is requested only when you tap "Start listening," and
  stops as soon as you tap "Stop" or leave that screen.

## Other data

- **Practice and lesson progress** (which checklist items and weeks you've
  completed) is stored locally on your device so it's there next time you
  open the app. It is not linked to your identity and is not shared with us.
- We do not use analytics SDKs, ad networks, or third-party trackers.
- We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other
  personal information.
- We do not show ads.

## Children's privacy

StringWise does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. Because no data leaves the device, there is nothing for
us to collect in the first place.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional
account, cloud sync, or purchases — this page will be updated and the "Last
updated" date above will change accordingly.
