# Privacy policy: StringWise

**Last updated:** 19 November 2026

This policy explains what StringWise does, and does not do, with your data. It applies to
StringWise version 2.3 and later.

## Microphone

StringWise uses your phone's microphone for the Tuner, the finger check on the Fingers
tab, Play-Along, and Record and Check.

- The microphone is used only while one of those features is listening, after you press
  Start listening or Start recording. It stops when you press Stop or leave the screen.
- Sound is analysed on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- The Tuner, the finger check and Play-Along do not record or save any audio.
- Record and Check keeps a short recording in the phone's memory while it checks your
  playing. The recording is deleted as soon as the check is finished. It is never saved
  as a file you can share and never leaves your phone.

## Other data stored on your phone

- Your lesson ticks, completed weeks, saved tempos, finished rhythms and settings are
  saved on your phone so they are there the next time you open the app. They are not
  linked to you and are not sent anywhere. You can clear them in Settings, or by
  uninstalling the app.

## What we do not collect

- StringWise has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, location,
  contacts or any other personal information.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and shows no ads.
- It does not need an internet connection and does not send data over the network.

## Feedback emails

If you choose Send feedback in Settings, your own email app opens with a message to us.
It includes the app version and your phone's model and Android version, which you can see
and remove before sending. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to
reply to you and to improve the app.

## Children

StringWise does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding accounts or online
features, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
